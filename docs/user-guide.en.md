# Using PriceTracker

[Português](user-guide.md) · [Getting started](getting-started.en.md)

## Update prices from Home

Home lists every available market, including newly registered chains. Open a chain to choose its
branches. **Update prices** saves that selection and takes you through three steps:

1. **Review your list**: add/remove products or update quantities; select **Confirm list and choose markets**.
2. **Confirm markets**: review branches, price source notes and round-trip tolls.
3. **Check prices now**: save the selection and start the background search, then watch progress.

You can also start directly from Markets. Adding a market as an administrator does not select it
for every account; each person chooses their own stores.

## Products and list quantities

Every catalog product can be edited with its pencil icon. Edits are private to your account. For
example, changing the egg count does not change everyone else's catalog. Create your own product
with **Create product**. Saving a new product returns to **My list**, where its card has **Add**;
adding it to a shopping list is a separate action. Use its pencil to edit it later or upload a photo.

A 1 kg package and a shopping quantity of 2 packs mean buying two 1 kg packages. Required word
groups are separated by commas: every group must match. Within a group, `|` lists alternatives.
Excluded words reject a listing. Search terms and matching words must use your markets' language,
even if you prefer the English interface.

## Double-ply toilet paper: lowest price per metre

In the list, find **Papel higiênico folha dupla** under **Hygiene** and press **Add**.
The default catalog item starts with **120 m** and accepts any pack size.
To create an independent version, select **Double-ply toilet paper** in **Create product**. The template sets required product/ply
words, allows any brand, selects **m (metres)** and enables **Compare any pack size by price per metre**.
Set the reference quantity to the metres you want (default: 120 m), create the product, then press
**Add** on its card. Your initial list quantity is those metres, rather than one roll or one pack.

A 4-roll × 30 m pack contains 120 m. A 12-roll × 30 m pack contains 360 m. At R$10 and R$24,
respectively, their unit prices are R$0.0833/m and R$0.0667/m: the larger pack is cheaper per metre.
Double ply does not double the metres. The matching rules exclude single ply and unrelated paper.
Listings without enough information to identify total length are excluded rather than guessed.

The source chooses its best equivalent listing by comparable price per metre. **Basket totals buy
whole packs** to cover your requested metres. The cheapest price per metre can therefore cost more
up front or buy extra metres; review the pack quantity and cost notes. Unit prices show four decimal
places so small differences remain visible. Availability still depends on each source's coverage.

To compare only one fixed pack size instead, turn off the any-size switch, enter its total metres and
choose a size tolerance. For an English-language store, adapt the search terms and required groups.

## Compare and track

**Common basket** compares only products found at every selected store. **By market** shows
coverage and missing items. **Budget plan** considers one store or splits across up to three stops,
with travel shown separately. Outdated or flagged prices are excluded by default.

**Price history → View table** supports sorting Date, Store, Listing, Price, Unit price and Status.
Click a header to sort ascending and again to sort descending. Arrows show the direction; missing
prices stay last. You can still scroll the table sideways on a phone or switch back to the chart.

Create a price alert from a product's edit page. Alerts use price per pack (including toilet paper),
per kg for weight products or per item. They are not per-metre target alerts. Schedules run the same
background collection; source health and item statuses explain blocked access or missing products.

## Language and limits

Choose Português (Brasil) or English in the sidebar, phone header or sign-in page. The choice persists
in this browser and switching language keeps open forms and the current page. Dates and numeric
formatting follow it. Currency stays BRL and scheduling uses Brasília time. Market names, source
listings, product names and search terms retain their original language.

Read [market sources](markets.en.md) for how prices are collected and what a new market registration
actually validates. If no prices arrive, check that stores are selected and the worker is running.
The administrator can inspect source health under Administration.
